ELIGIBILITY

Before you apply

  • Applicants should be 18+ or have applicable parental/guardian consent.
  • Provide complete and accurate information.
  • Only one application should be submitted per person.
  • Additional verification may be requested.
  • Submitting an application does not guarantee assistance.

Does submitting an application guarantee support?

No. An application is only an expression of interest and does not guarantee selection or assistance.

Is there a purchase requirement?

This prototype does not require a purchase to submit an application.

Will additional information be requested?

If a legitimate program were operating, verification requirements would depend on its published terms. Never provide passwords, PINs or payment credentials.
